Xin-shu-bao tablets ameliorates ventricular remodeling against HFrEF via PPARγ/MFGE8 pathway based on MALDI-MSI and lipidomics

Zhang, F., Li, Z., Zhang, Y., Yang, J., Xiao, H., Li, X., Yang, H.

Fourth author

Journal of Ethnopharmacology347, 119741 (2025)

DOI

Abstract

MALDI-MSI lipidomics and molecular biology show that Xin-shu-bao tablets ameliorate ventricular remodeling after HFrEF via PPARγ/MFGE8 signaling. The study combines spatial lipid mapping with targeted mechanistic experiments.
