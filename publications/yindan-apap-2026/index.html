Yindan Pinggan Capsules alleviates acetaminophen-induced liver injury by modulating ferroptosis via the system Xc−/GSH/GPX4 axis and the LPCAT3–ACSL4 pathway

Yang, J., Zhang, Y., Yang, R., Zhang, F., Jiang, X., Zhuang, Y., Li, X., Yang, H.

Co-first author

Science of Traditional Chinese Medicine (2026)

DOI

Abstract

We combined proteomic and transcriptomic profiling with functional assays to show that Yindan Pinggan capsules alleviate acetaminophen-induced liver injury by suppressing ferroptosis through the Xc−/GSH/GPX4 axis and the LPCAT3–ACSL4 pathway. The study defines a candidate mechanistic axis in preclinical models; clinical efficacy was not tested.
