Yin-Dan-Ping-Gan Capsule mitigates CCL4-induced liver fibrosis via regulating PPARγ/GPX4 signaling and suppressing ferroptosis

Jiang, X., Yang, J., Zhang, Y., Zhang, Y., Ouyang, Z., Zhao, C., Lin, L., Li, X., Huang, L.

Second author

Pharmaceuticals19, 251 (2026)

DOI

Abstract

Integrating proteomics, network pharmacology, and deep learning, we show that Yin-Dan-Ping-Gan capsules mitigate CCL4-induced liver fibrosis by activating PPARγ/GPX4 signaling and suppressing ferroptosis. The evidence is preclinical and identifies a candidate antifibrotic axis rather than a clinical therapy.
