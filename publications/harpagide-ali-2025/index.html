Harpagide confers protection against acute lung injury through multi-omics dissection of immune–microenvironmental crosstalk and convergent therapeutic mechanisms

Wang, H., Yang, J., Zhang, Y., Wang, J., Song, S., Gao, L., Liu, M., Chen, Z., Li, X.

Co-first author

Pharmaceuticals18, 1494 (2025)

DOI

Abstract

Multi-omics analysis of immune–microenvironmental crosstalk shows that harpagide protects against acute lung injury through convergent HIF-1α, Nrf2, and PI3K signaling. The study integrates single-cell transcriptomics with blood and lung proteomics in preclinical models.
