Protective effect of peony (Paeonia ostii) flower extract against tape stripping-induced skin barrier impairment in mice

Yang, R., Yang, J., He, G., Zhang, Y., Jiang, X., Wang, J., Yang, H., Shang, C.

Co-first author

Molecules31, 62 (2026)

DOI

Abstract

In a mouse tape-stripping model, peony flower extract restored skin barrier function and was associated with upregulation of multiple barrier-related proteins. The study links a proteomics-informed protein set to a measurable skin phenotype in mice.
