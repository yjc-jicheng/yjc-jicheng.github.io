Abstract

Proteomics, transcriptomics, and machine-learning simulations identify PI3K/AKT signaling as a central axis in LPS/D-GalN-induced acute liver injury and as a target of Yin Dan Ping Gan capsules. The work is accepted for publication; the full author list and DOI will be added when available.
